## What is Fibe?

Fibe gives your team instant, isolated full-stack environments powered by Docker. Whether you need a quick staging preview, a demo environment for a client, or an isolated sandbox for development, spin one up in seconds.

- **Product updates** — New features, improvements, and roadmap
- **Engineering deep dives** — How we built the platform
- **Guides** — Tips and tricks for getting the most out of Fibe

Stay tuned for more!
